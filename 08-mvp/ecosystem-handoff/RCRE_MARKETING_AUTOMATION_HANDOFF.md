## Durable contracts and action sequence

Content record: `id, organizationId, ownerId, officeId, version, title, body, channel, workflow, status, tags, audience, market, source, license, listingId?, assetIds?, scheduleAt?, approvedBy?, approvedVersion?`. Allowed statuses: draft/review/approved/scheduled/paused/canceled/archived. The API validates input and stale versions; the current generalized record is not yet a standalone reusable typed package.

Content-history record snapshots preserve prior versions. Outbox entry captures approvedVersion, full exact publication fields, SHA-256 payloadHash, scheduledAt and local state. Batch record stores owner/office and copied content IDs. `LibraryAsset` has file metadata, hash, version/previousId, owner/organization, rights declarations, optional related content and archive flag; bytes live in `runtime/library-assets`, not public assets.

Sequence: compose or upload → save editable local draft → submit/review exact content → authorized approval → schedule exact approved content → due local dispatcher → **ready for authorized external delivery**. A provider receipt would be a separate future transition. The current generic marketing dispatcher does not validate recipient consent, create channel credentials, send messages, publish posts, buy ads or infer a legal approval from the review state.

Permissions: agents own drafts and can read approved organization content; leaders/brokers edit their office; marketing_admin and broker_owner edit organization content. Copying approved content into a batch makes a new unapproved draft. Asset access follows ownership or an authorized referencing content record. Review policy is tenant-specific and must travel with extracted service logic.

## Reuse recommendation

Reusable now as a synthetic/local demonstration: MarketingComposer, MarketingBatches, LibraryAssets and marketing-mode workspace/API state machine (counted in the portal inventory, not additional components). Reuse difficulty: low for deterministic composer with injected copy/branding; medium for library/batches with storage and entitlement adapters; high for extracting the multi-mode workspace and approval service.

Candidate Skill: `realtor-marketing-system-builder`, limited initially to local content preparation, asset library, independent review and delivery preparation. Configuration inputs: tenant brand/disclosures, market vocabulary, role/office permissions, workflow templates, storage limits, approved asset rights, review policy and separate provider registry. Preserve confidential drafts, private assets, reviewer identities and approval history inside each tenant. Keep real listing/MLS data and imagery out unless separately licensed.

Required reproducibility tests: tenant/office/private draft denial; approved read-only reuse; asset ownership/signature/size and exact byte delivery; stale version conflicts; content/media edits invalidate approval/outbox; batch copies start unapproved; due dispatch idempotency; no provider call without exact approval; recipient consent/suppression/unsubscribe/provider receipt tests before adding actual delivery.

AI Realtor Pro presentation: **DEMO ONLY** for local reviewed campaigns; **NEEDS EXTRACTION** for reusable content library/marketing system; **NEEDS MORE DEVELOPMENT** for content automation with real external delivery; social publishing/ad launches **NOT READY**. Use synthetic content and explicit labels. Do not market deterministic templates as AI generation, local scheduling as publication, or rights metadata as a rights clearance service.

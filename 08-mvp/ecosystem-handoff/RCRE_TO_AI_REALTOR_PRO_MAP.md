## Approved direction for demonstrations, not publication authorization

1. Use an isolated synthetic tenant/configuration and neutral licensed assets. Do not point AI Realtor Pro visitors at RCRE's real local database, FUB mappings, conversations or private media.
2. Show website templates in interactive previews; show portal state changes through a resettable synthetic fixture. Label local simulation and disconnected providers beside the action.
3. Screenshots/video/optional HeyGen narration may illustrate verified workflows. Remove names/photos unless independently licensed for that product; real agent permission for RCRE is not blanket AI Realtor Pro marketing permission. No private CRM/export/download content.
4. Feature pages can invite “request a custom build,” “join the Realtor community” and “request a team/brokerage consultation.” Each destination remains site-approved and consent-based; no automatic enrollment or cross-brand lead sharing.
5. Use separate readiness copy per feature: “local demonstration,” “custom implementation,” “requires your authorized provider.” Never summarize all as live AI automation.

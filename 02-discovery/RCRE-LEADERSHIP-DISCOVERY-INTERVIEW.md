# RCRE Leadership Discovery — Conversation Guide

**For:** Jeremy, sitting with RCRE ownership
**Format:** One conversation, 45–60 minutes. Two if it runs long — that is a good sign, not a problem.
**Companion:** [DISCOVERY-QUESTIONS.md](DISCOVERY-QUESTIONS.md) is the exhaustive internal checklist.
**This** is the conversation. Use that one afterwards to check what you missed.

---

## How to run this

**You are not collecting requirements. You are finding the pain.** The systems questions are here so
you leave with what you need, but they are the second half of the conversation, not the first.

A few things that make this work:

- **Let them talk.** The best material comes from the tangents. If they start telling a story about a
  deal that fell apart, that is the meeting working.
- **Ask "tell me about the last time that happened."** Specifics beat generalities every time. "Leads
  fall through the cracks" is useless. "We got a Zillow lead Saturday morning and nobody called until
  Monday" is a design input.
- **Write down their exact words**, especially complaints. Their phrasing becomes the recruiting copy
  later.
- **Do not pitch.** The moment you start selling, they stop telling you what is broken. §9 has
  concept probes — those are for the end, framed as questions, not promises.
- **It is fine to leave gaps.** Better to have a real conversation and follow up by email than to
  march through a questionnaire.

**Bring:** [RCRE-CURRENT-TECHNOLOGY-INVENTORY.md](RCRE-CURRENT-TECHNOLOGY-INVENTORY.md) (leave it with
them) and [RCRE-DISCOVERY-DOCUMENT-REQUEST.md](RCRE-DISCOVERY-DOCUMENT-REQUEST.md) (walk through it at
the end).

**The one thing you cannot leave without:** a clear answer on §12 — what the commercial relationship
is. It changes the architecture.

---

## 1 · How RCRE runs today (5 min)

Open wide. Let them describe their own business before you frame anything.

- **Tell me how RCRE operates today.** Walk me through it like I know nothing.
- Who does what? Who owns which part of the operation day to day?
- How many agents do you have right now, and where are they?
- Which markets are you actually in — and which are you *marketing* in? *(Their site markets 10
  counties across AL and FL, but listings appear in Palm Beach, St. Lucie, Charlotte and Homestead
  too. Worth asking why.)*
- Is Georgia real, in progress, or aspirational?
- What does the org look like? Teams? Team leads? Or does everyone report to a managing broker?
- Who handles admin, transaction coordination, and marketing today?

> *Listen for:* whether Birmingham and Jacksonville are one business or two. That has architectural
> consequences.

## 2 · What's working, and what drives you crazy (8 min)

**This is the most important section in the meeting.** Do not rush it.

- **What's working really well right now?** What would you not want anyone to touch?
- **What drives you crazy?**
- **What do your agents complain about?** Not what you think they should complain about — what do
  they actually say?
- **What do you personally spend time on that you wish someone else handled?**
- What takes far longer than it should?
- What do you find yourself explaining over and over?
- What breaks when you go on vacation?
- **What do you wish your agents did consistently that they just... don't?**
- If you could fix one thing about how this brokerage runs, what would it be?

> *Push for specifics.* "Give me an example from last week."

## 3 · Where things get lost (7 min)

Leads and recruits both leak. Find out where.

**Leads**
- Where do your leads come from? Rank them by volume.
- Walk me through what happens when a new lead comes in. Who touches it first?
- How does a lead get to an agent — round robin, geography, performance, whoever grabs it, your call?
- **How fast are you expected to respond? How fast do you actually respond?**
- How would you even know if a lead went unanswered?
- **Where do leads get lost?**
- What happens to a lead that doesn't convert in 90 days?
- Do you pay for leads? Zillow, Realtor.com, paid social? What does that cost, and does it work?

**Recruits**
- **Walk me through recruiting today, start to finish.** Where do candidates come from, who talks to
  them, what do you send, how long does it take?
- Where do you track it?
- **Where do recruits get lost?**
- How many agents did you bring on in the last year? How many left?

> *Listen for:* whether anyone owns response time. Almost nobody does, and it is usually the single
> biggest recoverable revenue leak.

## 4 · Why agents join, and why they leave (7 min)

This is the recruiting product's source material. Get their words verbatim.

- **Why do agents actually join RCRE?** In their words, not the brochure's.
- **Why do agents leave?**
- Who are you competing against for agents in Birmingham? In Jacksonville?
- What do those brokerages offer that you don't?
- What do you offer that they can't match?
- Are you recruiting experienced agents, new licensees, or both?
- **How does the split work? How much of that can be public?**
- Are there fees an agent should know about — desk, tech, E&O, transaction?
- Any legal constraints on who you can recruit from?
- Walk me through onboarding. Signed agreement to first closing — what happens, and what takes
  longest?
- **How long does it typically take a new agent to close their first deal here?**

> *If they can't answer the last one, note it.* Time-to-first-closing is the single best recruiting
> statistic a brokerage can publish, and most cannot measure it.

## 5 · The CRM question (7 min)

Handle this carefully — it determines whether Phase 1 is build, integrate, or neither.

- **Is there a CRM today? Which one?**
- Who set it up? Who administers it?
- **Be honest with me — do agents actually use it?** Or is it something leadership looks at?
- What percentage of your agents log activity in it?
- What do you use it for that works?
- What do you hate about it?
- If you could replace it tomorrow with no migration pain, would you?
- **Do agents keep their own database in their own tools?**
- **When an agent leaves, whose clients are those?** Is that written down anywhere?
- Where do website leads actually land — the CRM, an inbox, someone's phone?
- Could you pull an export of it if we needed one?

> *The critical distinction:* a CRM that exists but nobody uses is a **different problem** from no CRM
> at all. The first is an adoption problem; the second is an infrastructure problem. Do not let this
> stay vague.

## 6 · The website and the vendor relationship (5 min)

- The site is on Luxury Presence — how has that been?
- What do you pay, and when does it renew?
- Who can make changes? You, or do you go through their support?
- **Where do the leads from the site go?** Can you get them out?
- Who writes the blog? It's genuinely good — is that in-house, them, or an agency? What does it cost?
- Do you get reporting from them? Do you look at it?
- The "Get Cash Offer" button — where does that go?
- **Which MLSs are you a member of, in each state?** Who holds the IDX agreements?
- Anything you wish the site did that it doesn't?

> *Do not signal any intent to replace the site.* You are gathering facts. The current
> recommendation is to keep it (ADR-0005).

## 7 · The rest of the stack (6 min)

Fast pass. Hand them the inventory form for detail — this is just to find the load-bearing systems
and the sore spots.

- What email are you on — Google or Microsoft? Do agents get an @rcregroup.com address?
- Calendar — shared or everyone's own?
- Phones and texting. Is there a system, or is it everyone's cell?
- **If agents text clients from personal phones, how would you ever prove consent?**
- Transaction management — Dotloop, SkySlope, Brokermint, paper?
- E-signature?
- How do commissions get calculated and paid?
- What marketing tools do you provide? Who pays — brokerage or agent?
- Is anything automated today? Zapier, drip campaigns, anything?
- Where do documents live?
- **Of everything we just listed — what's the one system you'd most like to set fire to?**

## 8 · Compliance, training and how work gets checked (5 min)

- Who reviews marketing before it goes out? Is there a process, or does it depend?
- What requires broker approval today?
- Is there a handbook, a policy manual, an agent agreement? Can I see them?
- **Have you had any fair housing, advertising, or license issues?** Anything that makes you cautious?
- Different rules between Alabama and Florida that trip people up?
- What training exists today? Onboarding, ongoing, anything?
- Do agents actually attend?
- Is there a playbook — scripts, checklists, how-we-do-things?
- **When a new agent has a question about how something works here, who do they ask?**

> *That last question tells you whether a brokerage knowledge system has a real job to do.*

## 9 · Concept probes (8 min)

**Frame these as questions, not plans.** Say so out loud: *"I want to test some ideas — I'm not
promising any of this yet, I want to know what would actually matter to you."*

For each one worth exploring, get: **Would this matter? Who'd use it? How often? What would make it
useless? What would make you uncomfortable?**

---

**A. The agent's morning**
> *"What if every morning, before an agent even opened their laptop, they got a short summary — three
> new leads, two that never got followed up, a past client whose closing anniversary is next week, a
> buyer who's gone quiet for nine days — and it told them who to call and why?"*

**B. The broker's morning**
> *"What if you got one briefing each morning showing only the handful of things that actually need
> you? Twelve leads yesterday, median response 14 minutes, three never answered, four agents with
> overdue follow-ups, two recruits who just got very engaged."*

**C. An assistant that knows the database**
> *"What if an agent could ask, 'Who should I follow up with today?' — and it actually knew their
> database, their pipeline, and their history, instead of giving generic advice?"*

**D. The listing that builds itself**
> *"What if, the moment an agent takes a listing, the system produced the task list, the marketing
> plan, the open house plan, the social content, and the follow-up campaign — all drafted, all held
> for approval?"*

**E. An assistant that knows RCRE**
> *"What if every agent had an assistant that knew how RCRE actually operates — our policies, our
> forms, our process — so they stopped asking you the same questions?"*

**F. AI as the recruiting pitch**
> *"What if the thing that made agents want to join RCRE wasn't the split — it was that you give them
> technology no other brokerage in Birmingham or Jacksonville has?"*

**G. Teaching agents outside RCRE**
> *"What if we taught AI to real estate agents generally — not just RCRE's — and that became how we
> meet the ones worth recruiting?"*

---

> **Watch for hesitation as carefully as enthusiasm.** If B makes them uncomfortable — because it
> means measuring agents — that is important. If D excites them but E doesn't, that tells you where
> to start. **Note what they push back on.**

## 10 · Measuring the business (4 min)

- What do you look at weekly? Monthly?
- How do you put that together today?
- What do you measure per agent?
- **What report do you wish you had that you don't?**
- How do you know if an agent is struggling before they leave?
- Do you track where business actually comes from?

## 11 · Technology reality check (4 min)

- How comfortable are your agents with technology, honestly? Best and worst.
- **Who would refuse to use something new?**
- Is anyone using AI already? ChatGPT for listing descriptions, anything?
- Do they do it openly, or quietly?
- Do agents make their own marketing, or wait for the brokerage?
- If we gave everyone a new tool tomorrow, what percentage would actually adopt it?
- **Who are your two or three best agents to test something with?** People who'd give honest feedback
  and not just be polite.
- Would agents pay for premium tools, or does everything have to be included?

> *That "who would refuse" question is worth more than it sounds.* Adoption is the top delivery risk
> on this project.

## 12 · Scope, money and the partnership (5 min)

**Do not skip this. It changes the architecture.**

- What's the budget picture for this? Is there a number, or are we figuring that out?
- What's driving the timeline? Anything with a date on it — recruiting season, a contract renewal, a
  competitor?
- **What does success look like at 90 days? At a year?**
- Who at RCRE will I be working with week to week?
- **How do you see this working between us commercially?** Equity, fee, partnership, licensing?
- **Is this RCRE's system — or is there a world where this becomes something other brokerages use
  too?**
- Anything I should know that we haven't covered? Anything that's a hard no?

> **Why the second-to-last question is architectural:** a single-brokerage system and a product other
> brokerages license are materially different builds. Answering it now avoids rebuilding later
> (ADR-0003).

---

## Closing

1. Hand over **RCRE-CURRENT-TECHNOLOGY-INVENTORY.md** — *"Whoever knows the systems best, fill in
   what you can. Blanks are fine."*
2. Walk through **RCRE-DISCOVERY-DOCUMENT-REQUEST.md** — start with the five REQUIRED items.
3. Ask for the **agent roster** specifically. *(The website shows 13 agent pages and 9 on the
   homepage — worth resolving, and it may be a compliance issue on its own.)*
4. Ask permission to talk to **two or three agents directly**. What agents say differs from what
   leadership thinks they'd say, and both are useful.
5. Agree a follow-up.

## Immediately after — while it's fresh

Write down, before you do anything else:

- **Their exact words** on what drives them crazy, and on why agents join and leave
- The one thing they'd fix
- What they got visibly excited about in §9 — and what made them uneasy
- Anything that contradicts an assumption in
  [ASSUMPTIONS-REGISTER.md](ASSUMPTIONS-REGISTER.md)
- **Your read on the CRM question** — build, integrate, or neither
- **Your read on §12** — is this RCRE's system or a product?

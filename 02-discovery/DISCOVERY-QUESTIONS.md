# RCRE Discovery — Questions for Leadership

**Status:** Open. This is the gate before any build work begins.
**Date:** 2026-08-19
**Format:** Grouped by theme. **P1** questions block or reshape Phase 1. **P2** shapes phases 2–4.
**P3** is useful background.

Answers should be recorded in `02-discovery/ANSWERS.md` as they arrive, with the date and who
answered. Where an answer is not yet available, the working assumption is logged in
[ASSUMPTIONS-REGISTER.md](ASSUMPTIONS-REGISTER.md).

---

## A. Business, structure, and scale

| # | Priority | Question |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | **P1** | How many licensed agents are with RCRE today, in each state? The site shows 13 agent pages and 9 on the homepage — which is accurate? |
| A2 | **P1** | What is the target agent count in 12 and 24 months? This sizes everything. |
| A3 | **P1** | What is the legal/brokerage structure — one entity across AL and FL, or separate entities per state? Who is the qualifying broker in each? |
| A4 | **P1** | Is **Georgia** an actual operating market, in progress, or aspirational? |
| A5 | P2 | Are there teams within the brokerage, or do all agents report directly to a managing broker? |
| A6 | P2 | Is there an existing ISA, admin, transaction coordinator, or marketing staff? How many? |
| A7 | P2 | 2026 transaction volume and GCI, by state — even approximate. Needed to size the CRM and to write honest recruiting copy. |
| A8 | P2 | Is there a commercial/investment side, property management, or is this purely residential? Listings appear in markets (Port Charlotte, Homestead) well outside the marketed counties. |
| A9 | P3 | Who exactly are the decision-makers on this project, and who has budget authority? |

## The eight that matter most

If only one meeting is available, ask these:

1. **C1** — Is there a CRM today, and is it actually used?
2. **B1** — Walk me through recruiting today, step by step.
3. **B5** — What is the commission structure and how much can be public?
4. **E1 / E2 / E3** — What do agents complain about, what eats leadership's time, what should be automated?
5. **D1 / D2** — Where do leads come from, and how are they routed?
6. **C4** — Luxury Presence contract terms and renewal date.
7. **F1 / F4** — Who does compliance review, and what do the MLS rules allow?
8. **H3** — What is the commercial relationship between Jeremy and RCRE?

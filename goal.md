# RCRE Full Build Goal

## Mission

Take the existing RCRE project from its current state to the strongest functional, polished, demo ready and pilot ready product that can be built locally with the information, code, research, architecture, tests and assets already available.

This is an execution goal, not another planning phase.

Use the entire RCRE folder as the source of truth. Read the relevant existing research, ADRs, requirements, leadership feedback, Hermes research, Follow Up Boss work, demo code, MVP code, website audit, roadmap and tests before making material decisions.

Do not restart the project. Reuse what is good, replace what is weak, and finish the product.

## Hard Workspace Boundary

All project work must remain inside RCRE. Everything outside RCRE is READ ONLY. Do not write project files to Desktop, Documents, Downloads, /tmp, another project, or another location. Do not modify the live RCRE website or any production system without explicit Jeremy authorization.

## Operating Mode

Act as lead engineer, product designer, QA lead and technical program manager. Use as many parallel subagents as Claude Code can safely coordinate when workstreams are genuinely independent.

Delegate aggressively for frontend and UX, Follow Up Boss, Hermes and MCP, reporting, recruiting, marketing, training, website and SEO, testing, security, and responsive QA.

Do not create agent theater. Coordinate dependencies and integrate everything into one coherent product.

Do not stop after every small milestone. Make reasonable reversible local development decisions yourself.

Stop and ask Jeremy only for a genuine blocker involving credentials, production access, irreversible external actions, money, legal or compliance policy, brokerage policy that cannot responsibly be inferred, destructive changes, real customer communications, or anything explicitly requiring Jeremy approval.

## Product Thesis

RCRE is the proving ground for a future brokerage technology platform.

Follow Up Boss remains the incumbent CRM during the MVP. RCRE adds the intelligence, accountability, AI, automation, recruiting, marketing, training and management layer around it.

Hermes is the preferred personal AI runtime. The RCRE backend remains authoritative for identity, permissions, brokerage data, compliance, approvals and audit.

The three core AI functions confirmed by RCRE leadership are:

1. Lead Follow Up and Prioritization
2. Pipeline and Accountability Management
3. Personalized Business Coach and Assistant

Protect these priorities from feature creep.

## Definition of Done

The goal is complete only when Jeremy can open one local URL and confidently demo the complete RCRE technology story without apologizing for unfinished UX.

### Public and Login Experience

Deliver a polished RCRE technology and recruiting experience communicating systems and structure, coaching and mentorship, multi market opportunities, business growth, accountability, training, education and RCRE AI.

Provide polished demo Agent and Managing Broker login paths. All business data must be synthetic and clearly demo data without ugly developer banners.

### RCRE Today

Make this the agent's daily operating screen. It must answer who to contact, why, what has not been followed up, what is overdue, what is sitting too long, what appointments matter, the three highest priority actions, how to time block the day, and what RCRE AI can prepare or handle.

### RCRE AI

Deliver a convincing agentic assistant demo that visibly understands CRM and pipeline context.

Core flows: Who should I contact today? Why? Draft the text. Plan my day. Show leads nobody followed up with. Show my pipeline. Handle what you can. What needs my approval?

Clearly distinguish completed, drafted and approval required actions. Never imply scripted behavior is live AI when it is not.

### CRM Intelligence

Deliver a polished intelligence layer over Follow Up Boss concepts including contacts, search, filtering, source, stage, assigned agent, last touch, next action, AI priority, contact workspace, timeline, property interest, tasks, appointments, deal context, AI insight and recommended action.

Every meaningful row opens the correct record.

### Pipeline

Show the real estate book of business clearly with appropriate stages, aging, overdue follow up and next actions.

### RCRE Command and Reporting

Command is exception first. It must answer which leads, agents, pipeline stages and recruiting opportunities need attention.

Detailed reporting one level deeper should support agent, source, stage, market and date range, with assigned leads, response time, contact attempts, appointments, conversion, stage aging, contracts, closings and fallout where data supports them.

### Recruiting

Deliver a recruiting operating concept with prospect pipeline, acquisition source, AI training engagement, website engagement, last contact, recommended next action, recruiting priority and movement toward onboarding.

Demonstrate future acquisition through referrals, AI education, Facebook, Instagram and YouTube, not only cold ISA calls.

### Marketing

Show RCRE agent marketing for listing campaigns, social, open houses, email, video, buyer and seller campaigns, database and past client campaigns, and personal branding. At least one campaign workflow must be convincingly interactive end to end.

### Listings

Provide a polished listing workspace with property context, marketing status, tasks, engagement and AI campaign generation.

### Training

Provide a polished RCRE AI Academy demo showing structured Realtor AI education and progress without building an oversized LMS.

### Website Strategy

Maintain an implementation ready backlog for buyer leads, seller leads, recruiting, agent showcase, local and technical SEO, AEO, generative search visibility, conversion, Follow Up Boss integration and attribution. Do not rebuild the live Luxury Presence site during this goal.

### Follow Up Boss

Finish the local integration architecture and make it pilot ready. Support verified API and webhook patterns, historical backfill design, event ledger, normalization, first touch, stage history, assignment history and reporting derivations.

Production remains read only until Jeremy authorizes otherwise. Do not fabricate SMS read receipts.

### Hermes and MCP

Deliver pilot ready RCRE Hermes assets using supported extension mechanisms. No Hermes core fork.

Provide agent and broker profiles, focused RCRE skills, controlled MCP tools, server side authorization, approval gates, audit behavior and PII memory protections. Do not build seven separate bots.

### UX Quality

Light mode is default and dark mode remains available. Use RCRE's verified identity. Every major route must be polished on desktop and mobile.

No dead navigation, fake buttons, ugly debug controls, generic AI styling, or broad redesign after the approved direction unless required to solve a real UX problem.

### Security and Compliance

Maintain organization scoped data, RLS architecture, least privilege, server only secrets, read only production defaults, approval gates, audit logging, no unrestricted database MCP tool, no PII in Hermes long term memory, no autonomous high risk brokerage actions, and no fabricated compliance claims.

### Testing

Before completion verify typecheck, production build, existing MVP tests, permissions, MCP authorization, FUB normalization, webhook idempotency, insight calculations, responsive routes, critical click paths, and agent and broker demo flows.

Fix failures before claiming completion.

## Execution Priorities

Prioritize work that improves end to end demo quality and pilot readiness.

Do not spend substantial time creating more strategy documents unless required to implement or safely operate the product. Do not continue external design research unless a concrete design problem cannot be solved from existing research.

Prefer working software over documentation volume. Prefer one excellent flow over five half built flows. Prefer real interaction over decorative UI. Prefer technical enforcement over prompt based safety.

# RCRE Procedures

> **PLACEHOLDER — knowledge base not yet connected.**
>
> RCRE's policy manual, agent handbook and transaction checklists have been
> requested but not supplied (discovery items 7 and 13). Until they exist in the
> RCRE knowledge store, this skill has nothing authoritative to retrieve.

## When to use
"What's our policy on…" · "How do I…" · "What form do I need for…"

## Procedure — current
1. Recognise that the knowledge base is not yet connected.
2. **Say so and direct the agent to their broker.**
3. Note the question. Recurring questions are the highest-value candidates for
   the knowledge base, and collecting them costs nothing.

## Procedure — once connected
1. Retrieve from the RCRE knowledge store.
2. Answer **with a citation** to the source document and section.
3. Escalate genuinely novel questions to the broker rather than reasoning from
   first principles.

## Pitfalls
- **Never invent a brokerage policy.** A confident wrong answer about a
  compliance requirement is worse than no answer, because the agent will act on
  it.
- Alabama and Florida differ. Never generalise one state's rule to the other.
- Never give legal advice. Contract *explanation* with a citation is acceptable;
  advising what to do is not.
- Never answer a compliance question from general real estate knowledge and
  present it as RCRE policy.

## Verification
Either the answer carries a citation, or it says the knowledge base is not
connected. There is no third option.

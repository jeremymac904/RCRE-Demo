# Negotiation and Client Experience

## Negotiation Is More Than Price

The supplied Realtor Knowledge Book identifies negotiation as a major skill gap.

A strong agent understands interests, leverage, alternatives, timing, evidence, emotion, and communication.

## Seller Side Negotiation

Understand:

Seller goal.

Net requirement.

Timing.

Risk tolerance.

Buyer strength.

Comparable sales.

Competing offers.

Appraisal risk.

Inspection risk.

Financing risk.

Closing terms.

The agent should help the seller compare the whole offer.

## Buyer Side Negotiation

Understand:

Buyer ceiling.

Monthly payment sensitivity.

Cash available.

Competing properties.

Market competition.

Seller motivation when known.

Appraisal risk.

Inspection priorities.

Closing flexibility.

Use alternatives as leverage when real alternatives exist.

Do not bluff about another property or competing offer.

## Communication

Strong negotiators do not create unnecessary enemies.

Maintain professional communication with the other agent.

Ask questions.

Clarify the issue.

Present evidence.

Protect the client's confidential information.

Avoid emotional escalation.

## Client Experience

The CORE and contract to close material emphasizes proactive communication.

The client should know:

What has happened.

What happens next.

What is needed from them.

What the risk is.

Who owns the next action.

When they will hear from the agent again.

## Weekly Update Standard

For active transactions, a structured update may cover:

Current status.

Upcoming deadline.

Financing.

Inspection.

Appraisal.

Title.

Repairs.

Client action.

Next communication.

The exact content depends on the transaction.

## Referral Through Service

The supplied coaching material emphasizes asking for referrals after clear service wins.

Examples:

A difficult issue is resolved.

The client praises communication.

The negotiation goes well.

The property launches successfully.

Closing is on track.

The client expresses gratitude.

The best referral system is exceptional service plus a professional request.

## Review

After closing, review:

What worked.

What created stress.

What the client valued.

What should be improved.

Was a review requested?

Was a referral requested?

Was the client placed into the long term database plan?

## Rule

Client experience is not separate from lead generation.

Great service creates the next transaction.

# Team Leadership and Profitability

## Scale Only After The Model Works

The supplied coaching material emphasizes leverage through assistants, buyer agents, systems, and leadership.

Current Tom Ferry Elite and Team coaching also emphasizes systems, referral business, technology, AI, and scalable growth.

A coach should not recommend hiring simply because the agent is busy.

First determine whether the business can afford the role and whether the role solves the real bottleneck.

## First Leverage

Common first leverage can include:

Transaction coordination.

Administrative support.

Marketing support.

Showing support.

The correct role depends on what removes low value work from the agent while protecting client experience.

## Role Design

Every role needs:

Purpose.

Responsibilities.

Success metrics.

Weekly priorities.

Communication rhythm.

Training plan.

Decision rights.

Compensation structure reviewed for legal compliance.

## Profit Versus Production

An agent can increase volume while reducing quality of life and profit.

Do not coach only toward higher GCI.

Ask:

What does the owner keep?

How many hours are required?

Which lead sources are profitable?

Which team roles create leverage?

Which expenses are producing a return?

Where is waste?

## Red Light Green Light Review

The supplied coaching material uses a practical expense review mindset.

Green light:

Expense has clear value or measurable return.

Yellow light:

Value is uncertain and requires review.

Red light:

Expense is unused, duplicated, or not producing value.

Review technology subscriptions regularly.

## Hiring Trigger

A hire may make sense when:

The work is repetitive.

The work can be documented.

The agent is consistently doing lower value tasks instead of revenue work.

The role can be funded.

The role protects or improves client experience.

## Rule

Build a business that can produce profit without requiring the owner to personally do everything.

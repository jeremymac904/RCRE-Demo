# Lead Generation System

## Principle

Real estate is a relationship and conversation business supported by media and technology.

A lead generation system should create a predictable flow of new conversations and future opportunities.

## Choose Two To Four Pillars

The supplied coaching library and current Tom Ferry Core model strongly support focus.

High value pillars include:

Database.

Referrals.

Geographic farm.

Open houses.

Social media.

Video.

Direct prospecting.

FSBO.

Expired.

Builder relationships.

Local business relationships.

Agent referrals.

Community events.

Paid digital leads.

Seminars.

Investor outreach.

The correct mix depends on the agent.

## Pillar Selection Test

Score each potential pillar on:

Current skill.

Market fit.

Enjoyment or tolerance.

Cost.

Speed to opportunity.

Long term compounding value.

Database growth.

Listing potential.

Repeatability.

Choose the strongest combination.

## Lead Pillar Design

Every pillar needs:

Target audience.

Offer.

Conversation opener.

Lead capture method.

Follow up plan.

Appointment goal.

Weekly activity target.

Conversion metric.

Cost metric if paid.

## Database Pillar

Goal:

Create repeat and referral business.

Weekly actions:

Personal outreach.

Homeowner value conversations.

Life event follow up.

Referral requests.

Review requests.

Client event invitations.

## Open House Pillar

Goal:

Create buyer and seller appointments.

Actions:

Choose strategically.

Promote locally.

Invite neighbors.

Invite database.

Use social content.

Capture contact information.

Conduct discovery.

Set next steps before the visitor leaves when appropriate.

Follow up quickly.

## Geographic Farm Pillar

Goal:

Become the recognized local expert.

Actions:

Consistent direct outreach.

Market updates.

Local video.

Neighborhood events.

Listings.

Open houses.

Community stories.

Home value conversations.

Reviews from clients in the area.

## Social Media Pillar

Goal:

Create awareness, trust, conversations, and database growth.

Actions:

Local short video.

Educational content.

Personal brand content.

Proof.

Stories.

Direct message follow up.

Lead magnets.

## Direct Prospecting Pillar

Goal:

Create immediate conversations.

Targets may include:

Expireds.

FSBOs.

Circle prospecting.

Past leads.

Old clients.

Rental owners.

Builders.

Local businesses.

The specific tactic should comply with current laws and calling rules.

## Measure By Source

For each pillar track:

Attempts.

Conversations.

Leads.

Appointments.

Signed clients.

Closings.

GCI.

Cost.

Time.

A lead source that creates many leads but few closings may be weaker than a source with fewer leads and stronger conversion.

## Rule

Do not add a fifth lead source because the first four became boring.

Improve execution before adding complexity.

# Content and Marketing Engine

## Marketing Is A System

Current Tom Ferry 2026 marketing guidance argues that many agents have a visibility problem rather than simply a lead problem.

A complete marketing system has six parts.

Audience.

Offer.

Message.

Distribution.

Capture.

Follow up.

## Audience

Define who the content is for.

Examples:

Homeowners in a farm.

Move up buyers.

First time buyers.

Investors.

Military relocation clients.

New construction buyers.

Luxury sellers.

Do not use protected classes as targeting or exclusion criteria.

## Offer

The offer gives the audience a reason to engage.

Examples:

Home value review.

Buyer planning session.

Neighborhood guide.

Moving guide.

Seller preparation checklist.

New construction list.

Investment property review.

Market report.

## Message

Answer a real problem.

Good messages are:

Specific.

Local.

Useful.

Credible.

Easy to understand.

Connected to the agent's experience.

## Distribution

Use channels intentionally.

Social media for discovery.

Video for trust.

YouTube for search and depth.

Email for owned audience.

Google for local discovery.

Direct mail for farm repetition.

Events for relationships.

Paid ads for scale when economics work.

## Capture

Do not send every person to the home page.

Use:

Direct message keyword.

Simple form.

Calendar.

Landing page.

Text response.

QR code.

Open house sign in.

CRM entry.

## Follow Up

The lead should enter a relevant follow up path.

Hot buyer.

Future buyer.

Homeowner.

Seller lead.

Open house visitor.

Past client.

Referral partner.

Local business.

Each needs different follow up.

## Content Calendar

A simple month can include:

Local market update.

Neighborhood tour.

Client story.

Buyer tip.

Seller tip.

Listing story.

Local business feature.

Behind the scenes.

FAQ.

Opinion.

Myth correction.

Call to action.

## Campaign Thinking

Instead of publishing one listing post, build a listing campaign.

Possible assets:

Coming soon.

Property story.

Walkthrough.

Neighborhood feature.

Open house.

Agent outreach.

Email.

Short clips.

Seller education tied to the launch.

Just sold story.

Case study.

## Metrics

Cost.

Reach.

Leads.

Database additions.

Appointments.

Closings.

GCI.

Profit.

A marketing system should eventually be judged by business results.

# Prospecting and Conversations

## The Goal Of Prospecting

The goal is not to complete calls.

The goal is to create meaningful conversations and logical next steps.

The supplied coaching material consistently emphasizes scripts, role play, prospecting, discovery, and appointment setting.

## Conversation Flow

Use a flexible pattern.

1. Open.

2. Establish relevance.

3. Discover.

4. Clarify motivation.

5. Understand timing.

6. Identify obstacles.

7. Offer value.

8. Set a logical next step.

## Discovery

Useful categories include:

Who is involved?

What do they want?

Where do they want to go?

When do they want to move?

Why does it matter?

What is stopping them?

How much can they invest or qualify for when relevant?

What has already happened?

## Make Resistance Safe

The supplied Realtor Knowledge Book describes a useful principle from inside sales coaching.

When resistance appears, first make the prospect right.

Do not argue.

Example:

Prospect says, We are waiting for rates to drop.

Poor response:

Rates might not drop.

Better response:

I understand why you are watching rates. A lot of buyers are. What rate or payment would make you comfortable enough to move?

The objection becomes discovery.

## Logical Next Step

Every real lead conversation should move toward a defined next action.

A strong next step includes:

When.

Why.

What value the prospect receives.

Examples:

Buyer consultation on Thursday at 4 PM to review financing, representation, and available homes.

Seller meeting Saturday at 10 AM to review pricing, timing, and marketing.

Call next Tuesday after the lender reviews income documentation.

## FSBO Principle

The supplied Mike Ferry and Tom Ferry style material frames FSBO conversion around motivation, timing, current marketing, price, and what would need to happen before the seller considers professional representation.

Do not insult the seller for trying on their own.

Understand why they chose it.

Create value.

Earn the appointment.

## Expired Principle

Do not lead by attacking the previous agent.

Understand:

Where they were moving.

Why they wanted to move.

What happened.

What feedback they received.

What they would expect from the next agent.

What would need to change for them to relist.

## Follow Up

The supplied coaching material supports persistent, structured follow up.

Follow up should have a reason.

Bad:

Just checking in.

Better:

You told me you wanted to move before school starts. I pulled the newest homes in the two neighborhoods we discussed. Can we review them today at 5 or tomorrow at 10?

## Role Play

Practice:

Opening.

Discovery.

Appointment close.

Price objection.

Commission objection.

Lender objection.

Timing objection.

Existing agent objection.

Spouse objection.

Fear.

Indecision.

The objective is not robotic memorization.

The objective is calm competence.

# Local Authority, Google, and AI Search

## The New Discovery Environment

Consumers can discover agents through:

Google.

Maps.

Reviews.

Social media.

YouTube.

Real estate portals.

Referrals.

AI generated answers.

A modern agent should build a consistent digital identity across these surfaces.

## Google Business Profile

Google describes local ranking primarily through:

Relevance.

Distance.

Prominence.

Agents should keep business information accurate and complete.

Useful areas include:

Business category.

Description.

Contact information.

Hours.

Photos.

Video.

Reviews.

Responses to reviews.

Services where appropriate.

Posts and updates where available.

Because Google product features can change, verify current options before coaching detailed setup.

## Review System

A strong review system includes:

Ask at a moment of value.

Send the direct link.

Make the request personal.

Follow up once if needed.

Respond professionally.

Repurpose positive feedback with permission.

Never fabricate a review.

Do not coach clients on what rating to give.

## Searchable Expertise

The agent should consistently associate their name with:

Market.

Neighborhoods.

Property type.

Consumer problem.

Niche.

Expertise.

Examples:

Jacksonville military relocation Realtor.

St Johns County new construction Realtor.

Orange Park listing agent.

Local investor Realtor.

Use only niches that are lawful and not based on exclusion of protected classes.

## AI Search Visibility

AI systems increasingly synthesize information from web sources.

Agents can improve discoverability by building real public evidence of expertise.

Useful signals may include:

Consistent profiles.

Accurate website.

Google reviews.

Local pages.

YouTube.

Local media.

Community partnerships.

Useful articles.

Citations from other websites.

Published market commentary.

The goal is not to manipulate AI.

The goal is to create enough credible information that search systems can understand who the agent is and what they know.

## Local Authority Content

Publish:

Neighborhood guides.

Market updates.

Price point examples.

New construction updates.

Local business interviews.

Moving guides.

Buyer education.

Seller education.

Case studies.

Community event coverage.

## Authority Stack

A strong stack may include:

Website.

## Rule

Authority is the accumulated result of useful public evidence, not a slogan.

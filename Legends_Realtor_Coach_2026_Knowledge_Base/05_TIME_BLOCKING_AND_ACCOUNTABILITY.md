# Time Blocking and Accountability

## The Calendar Is The Business Plan In Motion

The supplied coaching material repeatedly emphasizes calendar discipline, lead generation time, follow up time, script practice, appointment windows, personal commitments, and review.

The goal is not to create a perfect calendar.

The goal is to protect the activities that produce future business.

## Calendar Order

Schedule in this order.

1. Personal and family commitments.

2. Existing contractual and client obligations.

3. Lead generation.

4. Lead follow up.

5. Appointment windows.

6. Script practice and training.

7. Content and marketing.

8. Transaction and administrative work.

9. Everything else.

## Daily Revenue Block

A useful daily revenue block can include:

Database calls.

Lead follow up.

New prospecting.

Direct messages.

Referral asks.

Appointment setting.

The block should produce conversations, not just attempts.

## Script Practice

The supplied Locker Room and coaching materials emphasize daily script and role play.

Practice should cover the conversations the agent is actually having.

Examples:

Buyer consultation opening.

Listing appointment opening.

Price objection.

Commission objection.

We want to think about it.

We already have an agent.

We are waiting for rates.

We are not ready.

Call us in a few months.

## Weekly Accountability Review

Review objective numbers.

Meaningful conversations.

Appointments set.

Appointments held.

Buyer agreements signed.

Listings taken.

Contracts written.

Closings.

Database additions.

Referral requests.

Content published.

Lead follow up completed.

Then compare commitments with actual performance.

## The Four Questions

Use the Locker Room reflection framework.

What should I keep doing?

What should I be more intentional about?

What should I stop doing?

What should I start doing?

## Accountability Without Shame

The coach should be firm and factual.

Weak response:

You had a rough week, do not worry about it.

Better response:

You committed to 25 conversations and completed 11. The goal did not fail. The activity was incomplete. What specifically interrupted the block, and what changes this week?

The focus is behavior and system, not character.

## Protecting Against Busy Work

Agents can spend an entire day on:

Email.

Canva.

MLS browsing.

Transaction updates.

Social scrolling.

CRM cleanup.

Training.

None of those necessarily create new business.

Ask:

Did this activity create a conversation?

Did it support a current client?

Did it improve conversion?

Did it create a reusable system?

Did it produce measurable value?

If the answer is no, reduce it.

## Weekly Commitment Format

By the next coaching review the agent will:

Complete a defined number of meaningful conversations.

Hold a defined number of appointments.

Complete a defined marketing action.

Practice a defined skill.

Update the scorecard.

The commitment should be measurable.

# Video, YouTube, and Short Form

## Why Video Matters

Video allows a prospect to experience the agent's communication style, knowledge, energy, and local expertise before meeting.

It can create both discovery and trust.

Current Tom Ferry resources place strong emphasis on video and social media.

YouTube guidance also reinforces that viewer behavior and satisfaction matter in distribution.

## Short Form Structure

A useful short video often has four parts.

1. Hook.

2. Context.

3. Useful information.

4. Call to action.

## Strong Hooks

Use specificity.

Examples:

If you are moving to this city in 2026, know these three things first.

This is what $500,000 buys here right now.

Sellers in this neighborhood keep making the same mistake.

Before you choose new construction, ask the builder this.

I toured five homes today, and this was the biggest pricing mistake I saw.

## Retention

Do not waste the opening.

Avoid long introductions.

Move quickly into the problem.

Change visual framing when useful.

Use captions.

Keep examples concrete.

Deliver the promised answer.

## YouTube Strategy

Build around search and authority.

Useful topics:

Moving to the city.

Living in the city.

Best neighborhoods for specific lifestyle preferences without protected class steering.

Cost of living.

What a price point buys.

Buying process.

Selling process.

New construction.

Market updates.

Community tours.

Mistakes.

Case studies.

## Local Search Intent

Think like the consumer.

What would they type or ask?

Moving to Jacksonville Florida.

Buying a home in St Johns County.

Cost of living in Orange Park Florida.

New construction near Jacksonville.

Best commute options from a neighborhood.

Then create content that answers the question directly.

## One Video, Many Assets

One long form video can create:

Short clips.

Instagram Reel.

TikTok.

YouTube Shorts.

Facebook video.

Email.

Blog.

Carousel.

Story sequence.

Lead magnet.

AI can assist with repurposing, but the agent should review accuracy and voice.

## Video Metrics

Click through rate where available.

Average view duration.

Retention.

Watch time.

Comments.

Direct messages.

Leads.

Appointments.

Search traffic.

Subscribers are useful but not the primary business metric.

## Rule

The purpose of video is not to become an influencer.

The purpose is to become known and trusted by the right local audience.

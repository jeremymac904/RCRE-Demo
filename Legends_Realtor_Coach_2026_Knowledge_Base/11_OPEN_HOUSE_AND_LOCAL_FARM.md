# Open House and Local Farm

## Open Houses Are A Lead Generation Event

The supplied Tom Ferry and CORE material treats open houses as an appointment opportunity, not simply a property showing.

Current Tom Ferry Core coaching still lists open houses as a primary lead pillar.

## Before The Open House

Choose the right property.

Promote it.

Invite the database.

Invite neighbors.

Create social content.

Create a property video.

Prepare sign in.

Prepare financing support when appropriate.

Prepare discovery questions.

Prepare follow up.

## Neighbor Outreach

Neighbor outreach has two goals.

Create attendance.

Create seller conversations.

A useful conversation asks:

Did you know the home is for sale?

Who do you know who may want to move into the neighborhood?

Have you considered moving?

What would your next move look like?

## During The Open House

Welcome.

Allow the visitor to experience the property.

Then discover.

Do they live nearby?

Are they actively buying?

How long have they been looking?

Are they working with an agent?

Have they spoken with a lender?

What do they like?

What is missing?

What is their timing?

Can you help with another property?

Can you schedule a consultation?

## Follow Up

Follow up quickly.

Reference something specific from the conversation.

Offer relevant value.

Set a next step.

Do not send only an automated property alert and call that follow up.

## Geographic Farm

A farm is not just postcards.

It is repeated local presence.

Use:

Market updates.

Door outreach.

Open houses.

Neighborhood videos.

Community events.

Local business features.

Home value reviews.

Direct mail.

Email.

Reviews.

Listings.

Sold stories.

Local social content.

## Becoming The Local Expert

Know:

Inventory.

Recent sales.

Price trends.

Days on market.

New construction.

Schools from approved public information sources.

Amenities.

Commutes.

Local businesses.

Community events.

Future development from verified sources.

Avoid steering and protected class based descriptions.

## Farm Metrics

Homes in farm.

Contacts in database.

Conversations.

Appointments.

Listings.

Closings.

Direct mail cost.

Event cost.

Content reach.

Reviews.

Share of transactions where measurable.

## Rule

A farm compounds only when the agent is consistent enough to become familiar.

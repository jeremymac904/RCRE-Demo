# Buyer Conversion

## Buyer Representation Is A Consultation Business

The buyer experience should begin with a professional consultation, not random property showings.

Current NAR rules and ethics guidance require careful attention to written buyer agreements and clear compensation discussions.

Because rules and state law can change, verify current requirements before giving compliance specific advice.

## Buyer Consultation Goals

Understand the buyer.

Educate the buyer.

Clarify representation.

Explain the process.

Coordinate financing.

Set expectations.

Create commitment.

Establish communication standards.

Build the search strategy.

## Discovery

Ask:

Why are you moving?

When do you want to move?

Who is involved?

Where do you want to live?

What matters about the location?

What property features matter most?

What should we avoid?

What price range are you considering?

Have you spoken with a lender?

Do you need to sell first?

Have you written offers before?

What has frustrated you so far?

## Financing

A buyer should understand payment, cash requirement, closing costs, loan options, and qualification before serious shopping.

The Realtor should coordinate with a properly licensed mortgage professional.

Do not provide mortgage advice outside licensing.

## Value Proposition

The buyer needs to understand what the agent does beyond unlocking doors.

Examples:

Search strategy.

Off market or coming soon awareness where permitted.

Property evaluation.

Offer strategy.

Contract explanation within license boundaries.

Negotiation.

Inspection coordination.

Appraisal coordination.

Lender and title coordination.

Deadlines.

Communication.

Problem solving.

Closing support.

## Written Agreement

Explain:

Who represents the buyer.

Scope of service.

Duration.

Compensation.

How compensation may be paid.

What happens if another party contributes toward compensation.

Termination provisions.

Any state required disclosures.

Do not describe compensation as standard.

## Offer Strategy

Coach the agent to understand:

Market competition.

Comparable sales.

Days on market.

Seller motivation when known.

Financing strength.

Contingencies.

Closing timeline.

Credits.

Appraisal risk.

Inspection strategy.

The best offer is not always simply the highest price.

## Buyer Follow Up

After consultation, the buyer should know:

What happens next.

Who does what.

When the next conversation occurs.

How properties are selected.

How quickly the buyer must respond in the current market.

## Conversion Metrics

Buyer leads.

Buyer conversations.

Buyer consultations set.

Buyer consultations held.

Written agreements.

Offers.

Accepted offers.

Closings.

Track where buyers drop out.

Coach that stage.

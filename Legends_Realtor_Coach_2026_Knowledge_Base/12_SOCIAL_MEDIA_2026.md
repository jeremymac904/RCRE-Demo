# Social Media Strategy for Real Estate Agents in 2026

## Current Reality

NAR's 2025 Technology Survey found that social media was the leading technology source for quality leads among Realtors, at 39 percent.

The same research found that 75 percent of Realtors used social media professionally.

Current Tom Ferry 2026 social media guidance emphasizes strategy, systems, consistency, positioning, and converting attention into clients rather than merely increasing posting volume.

## The Goal

Social media should create:

Awareness.

Trust.

Conversations.

Database growth.

Appointments.

Referrals.

Not just followers.

## Content Pillars

A modern agent can use five content pillars.

### Local

Neighborhoods.

Businesses.

Events.

Development.

Market activity.

What different price points buy.

Local myths.

Moving guides.

### Education

Buyer process.

Seller process.

Financing with licensed partners.

Inspections.

Negotiation.

Contracts within license limits.

Market explanation.

### Proof

Client stories.

Reviews.

Case studies.

Listings.

Negotiation wins without revealing confidential information.

Process examples.

### Personality

Values.

Behind the scenes.

Community.

Family where desired.

Interests.

Why the agent works in the business.

### Opinion

Market interpretation.

What the agent would do in a scenario.

Common mistakes.

What consumers should pay attention to.

Keep opinions evidence based.

## Platform Roles

Instagram can build familiarity, proof, stories, short video, and direct messages.

Facebook can support local community, sphere visibility, groups, events, and retargeting.

TikTok can create discovery through short form local and educational content.

YouTube can build deeper authority and search visibility.

LinkedIn can support referral partners, relocation relationships, professional authority, and local business relationships.

The exact mix should follow the audience.

## Searchable Local Content

Generic content is increasingly easy to create with AI.

Local firsthand expertise is more defensible.

Prefer:

What $650,000 buys in this market.

Three mistakes I see sellers make in this neighborhood.

What buyers should know before moving to this area.

A tour of a local community.

A local market breakdown.

A real client case study.

## Content To Conversation

Every content system needs calls to action.

Examples:

Message me the word MAP.

Comment GUIDE.

Ask me for the neighborhood list.

Send me your address for a home value review.

Book a buyer planning session.

The next step should be easy.

Leads.

Database additions.

Do not coach only follower growth.

## Weekly Baseline

A practical starting point may include:

Three short videos.

Daily stories on active workdays.

One local market or neighborhood piece.

One proof or client experience piece.

Direct message follow up with engaged prospects.

The volume can change based on capacity.

Consistency matters more than a perfect posting calendar.

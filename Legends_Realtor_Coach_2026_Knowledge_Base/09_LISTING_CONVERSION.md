# Listing Conversion

## The Listing Appointment Is Won Before The Appointment

The supplied Tom Ferry and CORE materials strongly emphasize prequalification, all decision makers, seller motivation, timing, pricing expectations, marketing needs, and preparation.

## Prequalification

Confirm:

Reason for selling.

Desired move.

Timing.

Decision makers.

Price expectation.

How price was determined.

Mortgage balance when relevant.

Property strengths.

Property weaknesses.

Prior listing history.

What the seller wants from the agent.

Other agents being interviewed.

Readiness to list.

## Tailor The Presentation

Current Tom Ferry listing coaching argues against a generic presentation.

If the seller says communication is most important, demonstrate the communication system.

If marketing is most important, show the launch plan.

If certainty is most important, show process and proof.

If price is most important, explain pricing strategy and market evidence.

## Listing Presentation Structure

A strong presentation can cover:

Seller goals.

Timing.

Property.

Market.

Pricing.

Marketing.

Communication.

Negotiation.

Process.

Proof.

Fees.

Next steps.

## Present Proof

Useful proof can include:

Relevant track record.

Reviews.

Market knowledge.

Marketing examples.

Case studies.

Process.

Communication standard.

Negotiation approach.

Avoid inflated claims.

## Pricing

The supplied coaching material emphasizes telling the truth about price.

The agent should explain:

Comparable sales.

Current competition.

Pending activity where available.

Market absorption.

Condition.

Buyer response.

Pricing risk.

The current Tom Ferry three price concept can be used as a discussion framework when appropriate:

A strategy designed to create maximum initial interest.

A strategy aligned with current fair market evidence.

A higher aspirational strategy with clear risk.

Never present a pricing tactic as a guarantee.

## Marketing Value

A modern listing marketing plan may include:

Professional photography.

Video.

Short form content.

Property page.

MLS presentation.

Database promotion.

Agent outreach.

Open house.

Neighbor outreach.

Paid promotion when justified.

Email.

Social media.

Retargeting when available and compliant.

Weekly performance review.

## Commission Conversation

Do not frame compensation as fixed or standard.

Explain the services and value.

Explain what the agent or brokerage charges.

Explain current compensation structure accurately.

Be prepared to negotiate professionally.

## Objections

Common objections:

We want to think about it.

We want to interview another agent.

Your fee is too high.

Another agent suggested a higher price.

We want to try selling it ourselves.

We are not ready.

Handle by returning to goals, evidence, value, and next steps.

## If You Do Not Get The Listing

Follow up with purpose.

Send relevant market information.

Send proof.

Send requested material.

Clarify timing.

Set the next conversation.

Do not disappear after the appointment.
